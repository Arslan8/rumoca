## Evidence needed

Construct an otherwise-valid source-level witness, establish a clean baseline in a capable engine, then retranslate at the witness and compare with the runtime override. ## Original claim

`SI.Permeance` declares no `min` in `Units.mo`, and this declaration adds none of its own. It therefore accepts a negative value, and the magnetic analogue of conductance. It does **not** claim that any model sets such a value, or that anything fails. This is the weakest tier in this project and the one most likely to contain deliberate choices.
