## Original claim

`SI.Area` declares no `min` in `Units.mo`, and this declaration adds none of its own. It therefore accepts a negative value, and a negative cross-section is not a geometry. It does **not** claim that any model sets such a value, or that anything fails. This is the weakest tier in this project and the one most likely to contain deliberate choices.
