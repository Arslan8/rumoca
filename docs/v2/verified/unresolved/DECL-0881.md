## Original claim

`SI.Resistivity` declares no `min` in `Units.mo`, and this declaration adds none of its own. It therefore accepts a negative value, and a material property that is non-negative by definition. It does **not** claim that any model sets such a value, or that anything fails. This is the weakest tier in this project and the one most likely to contain deliberate choices.
